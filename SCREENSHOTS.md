# Screenshot provenance

These are PNG images directly extracted from test evidence in the supplied Batman M66 archive, not generated art.

- The title and five stage captures came from the bundled `EVIDENCE/FEATURES/release_campaign/` test captures.
- The stage high-score screen and score-QR example came from `SOURCE/EVIDENCE/FINAL_FEATURES/M65_*` fixtures.
- All captures were **160×152 native NGPC screen images**. Root `screenshots/*.png` are nearest-neighbour 3× enlargements; `screenshots/native/` contains the exact image pixels as captured.
- The game was in M65/M65R1 during these capture runs. M66 changed the score anti-farming calculations; it did not change the game artwork or gameplay scene renderer. Do not represent these images as fresh M66 real-hardware footage.
- These images and QR scores are examples/test fixtures, not verified real-player leaderboard submissions.
